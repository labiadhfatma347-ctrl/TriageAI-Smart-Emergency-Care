🧭 ABCDE Primary Assessment

ABCDE is a structured way of looking for immediately life-threatening problems. It is not a diagnosis.
A Airway

Is the airway open? Can the patient speak normally?

B Breathing

Look at the chest and check whether breathing is normal.

C Circulation

Look for major bleeding and signs of poor circulation.

D Disability

Check responsiveness and important neurological signs.

E Exposure

Look for important injuries or visible problems while protecting privacy and warmth.
