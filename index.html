🫁 ABCDE

A — Airway
Airway = the passage where air enters the lungs.
Check whether the person can speak and whether the airway appears blocked.
B — Breathing
Check whether breathing is normal. Gasping is not normal breathing.
C — Circulation
Check severe bleeding, pulse if appropriate, skin appearance and vital signs.
D — Disability
Check consciousness and neurological warning signs.
E — Exposure
Look for injuries, burns, bleeding or other visible problems while preserving privacy and warmth.

💡 Medical Terms Explained

SpO₂ = oxygen saturation, the percentage of oxygen carried in the blood.
Tachycardia = heart rate faster than expected.
Bradycardia = heart rate slower than expected.
Dyspnea = difficulty breathing / shortness of breath.
Unresponsive = the person does not respond normally when spoken to or stimulated.
Hemorrhage = significant bleeding.
RCP / CPR = cardiopulmonary resuscitation: chest compressions, with breaths when appropriate.
AED / DAE = automated external defibrillator, a device that analyzes heart rhythm and may deliver a shock.
FAST = Face, Arm, Speech, Time; a quick stroke warning check.

🚨 Emergency Mode

If a person is unresponsive and not breathing normally, treat this as a possible cardiac arrest.

⚠️ When to start CPR

CPR is for a person who is unresponsive and not breathing normally or is only gasping.

If the person is unconscious but breathing normally, CPR is not the appropriate action; place them safely on their side when appropriate and monitor breathing while getting emergency help.
