# AI Hospital Assistant 🏥🤖

An AI-powered solution designed to help emergency departments manage patient flow, prioritize cases, and reduce waiting times.

## 🎯 Goal

The project aims to support healthcare professionals by providing a smart preliminary assessment of patients based on their symptoms and pain level.

## 🚑 Main Features

- Smart patient pre-triage
- Pain assessment
- Priority level suggestion
- Emergency department flow management
- Support for healthcare professionals
- Reduction of unnecessary waiting times

## 💡 Innovation

The system combines Artificial Intelligence with emergency-care workflows to provide faster and more structured patient assessment.

## 🌍 Vision

To develop a practical AI solution adapted to Tunisian hospitals and help improve the quality and speed of emergency care.

## 🚀 Project Status

Prototype / Early Development

## 📌 Future Development

- AI symptom analysis
- Multilingual interface (Arabic / French / English)
- Real-time emergency department dashboard
- Patient monitoring
- Integration with hospital information systems

## 👩‍⚕️ Target Users

- Emergency departments
- Nurses
- Emergency physicians
- Hospitals and healthcare institutions
